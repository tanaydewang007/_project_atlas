# Residential Water Purifier Market 2025

[[page 1]]
## Executive Summary
Residential water purifiers treat household drinking water using filtration, ultraviolet disinfection and reverse osmosis. Rising awareness of waterborne disease and urban water quality concerns drive adoption. This CMR report sizes the global market and profiles technologies, channels and regions through 2030.

[[page 2]]
## Scope and Methodology
The market covers point-of-use and point-of-entry purifiers sold for household use, including consumables such as filters and membranes. Commercial and industrial treatment systems are excluded. Estimates are built from retailer sell-out data and manufacturer disclosures. The forecast period is 2025-2030.

[[page 3]]
## Market Drivers
Concern over contaminants in municipal supply, growing disposable incomes in emerging economies and the shift from boiled water to purified water support growth. Subscription-based filter replacement models improve revenue visibility for manufacturers.

[[page 4]]
## Market Size and Forecast
The global residential water purifier market was valued at USD 38.6 billion in 2024 and is expected to grow at a CAGR of 7.4% from 2025 to 2030. Asia-Pacific accounted for 46% of 2024 revenue.

[[page 5]]
## Technology Segmentation
Reverse osmosis leads in regions with high total dissolved solids in groundwater. Ultraviolet purifiers dominate where microbiological contamination is the main concern. Gravity-based and activated carbon systems serve price-sensitive segments.

[[page 6]]
## Distribution Channels
Offline retail and direct sales with installation remain important because of service needs. E-commerce is the fastest growing channel, helped by easy-to-install compact models and online filter subscriptions.

[[page 7]]
## Competitive Landscape
The market includes global appliance brands and strong regional specialists. Brand trust, service network reach and filter pricing determine loyalty. Smart features such as TDS displays and app alerts are becoming standard in premium models.

[[page 8]]
## Outlook and Recommendations
Manufacturers should invest in service networks, subscription consumables and energy-efficient RO designs that reduce rejected water. Retailers should bundle installation and annual maintenance to improve retention.
