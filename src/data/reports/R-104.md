# Global Industrial IoT Sensors Market 2025

[[page 1]]
## Executive Summary
Industrial IoT sensors monitor temperature, vibration, pressure and flow across factories, pipelines and utilities. Predictive maintenance and energy efficiency programmes are the strongest demand drivers. This CMR report sizes the global market and profiles technologies and regions through 2030.

[[page 2]]
## Scope and Methodology
The market covers sensor hardware and embedded firmware sold for industrial monitoring. Cloud platforms and integration services are excluded. The forecast period is 2025-2030.

[[page 3]]
## Market Size and Forecast
The global industrial IoT sensors market was valued at USD 18.9 billion in 2024 and is expected to grow at a CAGR of 9.1% from 2025 to 2030. Asia-Pacific accounted for 35% of 2024 revenue.

[[page 4]]
## Technology Trends
Wireless condition monitoring sensors with multi-year battery life are replacing wired installations in brownfield plants. Edge processing inside sensors reduces data volumes and enables local anomaly detection.

[[page 5]]
## End-Use Industries
Manufacturing, oil and gas, power generation and water utilities are the largest users. Food processing and pharmaceuticals show faster growth because of compliance driven monitoring.

[[page 6]]
## Outlook
Standardisation of data models and falling sensor prices will broaden adoption among mid-sized plants. Vendors that pair sensors with analytics subscriptions are best placed to capture recurring revenue.
