# Electric Vehicle Charging Infrastructure Market 2025

[[page 1]]
## Executive Summary
Electric vehicle charging infrastructure comprises chargers, networks and energy management software. Government incentives, vehicle adoption and grid investment drive growth. This CMR report sizes the global market and profiles charger types, business models and regions through 2030.

[[page 2]]
## Scope and Methodology
The market covers public and private chargers, installation and charging network software. Vehicle sales and battery manufacturing are excluded. The forecast period is 2025-2030.

[[page 3]]
## Market Size and Forecast
The global electric vehicle charging infrastructure market was valued at USD 31.4 billion in 2024 and is expected to grow at a CAGR of 22.5% from 2025 to 2030. Asia-Pacific accounted for 52% of 2024 revenue.

[[page 4]]
## Charger Segmentation
AC chargers dominate home and workplace installations, while DC fast chargers account for the growth in public corridors. Ultra-fast charging above 250 kW is emerging along highways and at fleet depots.

[[page 5]]
## Business Models
Operators earn revenue through per-kWh pricing, subscriptions and roaming agreements. Retail and hospitality sites host chargers to drive foot traffic, sharing revenue with network operators.

[[page 6]]
## Outlook
Grid capacity, permitting delays and interoperability will shape deployment speed. Operators with software-led energy management and reliable uptime will gain share.
