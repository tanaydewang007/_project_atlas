# Cloud Cybersecurity Services Market 2024

[[page 1]]
## Executive Summary
Cloud cybersecurity services help organisations secure workloads, identities and data in cloud environments. Rising attack volumes and compliance mandates drive spending. This CMR report sizes the global market and profiles service types and regions through 2030.

[[page 2]]
## Scope and Methodology
The market covers managed detection and response, cloud security posture management services, identity services and consulting. Pure product licences are excluded. The forecast period is 2025-2030.

[[page 3]]
## Market Size and Forecast
The global cloud cybersecurity services market was valued at USD 72.3 billion in 2024 and is expected to grow at a CAGR of 12.8% from 2025 to 2030. Asia-Pacific accounted for 24% of 2024 revenue.

[[page 4]]
## Service Segmentation
Managed detection and response is the fastest growing service line as organisations struggle to hire security analysts. Consulting and compliance assessment remain steady due to regulatory audits.

[[page 5]]
## Buyer Behaviour
Large enterprises consolidate vendors to reduce tool sprawl, while mid-sized firms prefer fully managed offerings. Financial services and healthcare are the most mature buyers.

[[page 6]]
## Outlook
Generative AI workloads, zero-trust architectures and data residency requirements will create new service demand. Providers that integrate automation to lower response times will differentiate.
